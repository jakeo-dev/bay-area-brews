# Version 4 Strategy

### Hypothesis

The most important change to make is to bottle a wider variety of potions and strategically offer them at certain times based on demand.

### Purchasing barrels

Barrels should be purchased based on which have the lowest cost per ml. The type of barrel doesn't need to be chosen strategically, since a wider variety of potions will be offered, but the junk barrel and barrels with a high cost per ml should be avoided. Buying the lowest cost barrels will save gold while maximizing ml.

### Selling potions

Potions should be offered depending on when demand for certain types is the highest. For example, if the red potion is most popular on Crownday, more potions with more red ml should be offered then. Right now, there are only nine possible potions that can be offered, so this should also be expanded on. This will increase gold by allowing customers to buy more of the potions that they are looking for.
